# Credit memo: Tembo Maize & Inputs

*Drafted by Insaka (llm:qwen/qwen3.8-27b); every figure links to its source. The RM reviews and decides.*

## Summary

The applicant is a maize and inputs trader in Eastern Province seeking [K25,000](#requested_amount) for [12](#tenor_months) months to buy seed and fertiliser. The business has traded for [7](#years_trading) years. The risk band is Low, with an estimated probability of default of [1%](#pd).

## Business and cash flow

The business shows [12](#months) months of statement history. Average monthly income is [K19,896](#mean_monthly_income), with a declared monthly turnover of [K20,000](#declared_monthly_turnover). Income peaks in [May 2026](#peak_month) at [K47,760](#peak_month_income), which is [2.8x](#peak_to_median) times a typical month. This pattern matches the expected season, with a season fit of [0.98](#season_fit). After adjusting for the season, income variation is [12%](#deseasoned_income_cv). The weakest month, adjusted for the season, is [January 2026](#weakest_month) at [80%](#weakest_month_ratio) of a typical month. Harvest-season income is [109%](#harvest_ratio) of the seasonal prediction.

## Repayment capacity

Average monthly committed costs are [K11,340](#mean_monthly_committed), leaving a surplus of [K8,557](#mean_monthly_surplus). The new loan instalment is [K2,667](#new_instalment). The surplus covers the instalment [3.2x](#debt_service_coverage) times. The account was overdrawn on [0%](#overdraft_day_share) of days.

## Risks and mitigants

The main risk factor is the lack of credit-bureau history, resulting in a thin file. Mitigants include the strong surplus cover for the new instalment, zero days in overdraft, and low income variation beyond the normal season. The largest customer accounts for [11%](#top_customer_share) of payments, and [57%](#mobile_money_share) of income is received via mobile money.

## Verification

All statements reconcile, and transfers match Absa's records. There are no rows needing review. PACRA and TPIN registration are declared but not yet checked with the relevant authorities. There is no credit-bureau hit.

## Recommendation for the RM

Verify PACRA and TPIN registration with the relevant authorities. Confirm the source of the mobile money income. Review the thin credit file to ensure no other obligations exist.
